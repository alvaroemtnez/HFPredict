# 0005. The app icon

Status: accepted (2026-11-11).

## Context

The first icon was a deep-sea disc with a lighter rim, a drop shadow, a
coverage field and a dashed distance ring. The station sat left of and below
centre and the field leaned north-east, so the desktop icon (which shows the
whole disc) looked shifted, while Android (which crops to its 72 dp window
and fills the rest with the rim colour) looked centred. The two differed.

Flat, single-mark concepts in the style of MeshConnect and Skypost (a white
glyph with cut-outs) were drawn and rejected: too symbolic for this app.

## Decision

- **Mark:** the Android icon's coverage field, unchanged in spirit: rounded
  cells in a three-step quality ramp around the station's dot, on a flat
  ground. No rim, no disc edge, no distance ring.
- **Centring:** on the field's bounding box; its half-extent is 64% of the
  visible icon on every platform.
- **One art everywhere:** Android, GNOME/Flatpak, AppImage, Windows and macOS
  show the same tile; only the corner shape changes (the launcher's mask, an
  80% rounded tile on the desktop).
- **Colourways:** Deep sea (`#0F2B3A`, teal ramp, amber station) is the
  default. On Android only, Preferences offers Night, Sunspot, Band open,
  Aurora and Daylight through launcher activity-aliases; the enabled alias is
  the only record of the choice.
- **Small sizes:** the symbolic icon and the status-bar icon use a coarse
  grid (cells twice the size), since the fine field turns to mush below 32 px.

## Consequences

`tools/gen-icons.py` writes every asset from one description: the desktop
SVG, the symbolic SVG, six adaptive icons, the themed layer, the status-bar
icon and the store `icon.png`. Never edit the generated files.
